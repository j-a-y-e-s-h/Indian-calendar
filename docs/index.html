Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Amanta and Purnimanta months?

In Amanta (prevalent in Gujarat, Maharashtra, South India), months conclude on Amavasya (New Moon). In Purnimanta (prevalent in UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, MP, Punjab, Delhi), months conclude on Purnima (Full Moon). The app provides a 1-tap toggle in Settings.

How are Choghadiya and Hora calculated?

Calculations are topocentric based on your exact latitude and longitude. The period from Sunrise to Sunset is divided into 8 equal parts for Day Choghadiya, and Sunset to next Sunrise into 8 equal parts for Night Choghadiya.

Does this app work without an internet connection?

Yes, 100% offline. The app includes a pre-compiled offline database of over 4,500+ global cities with coordinates and elevation. All astronomical trigonometry runs directly on your device CPU with zero network calls.

How do I pin transparent widgets to my Android home screen?

Long-press on your Android home screen, select Widgets, scroll to Vedic Hindu Calendar, and add the 4x4 Month Grid or 4x2 Daily Panchang widget. You can also tap "Pin Widget" directly inside the app's Settings modal.
