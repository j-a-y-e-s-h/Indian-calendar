# 🕉️ Vedic Hindu Calendar, Panchang & Kundali — Product Brief

## Product Overview
An offline-first, ad-free Android Vedic Ephemeris & Panchang application crafted with Material 3 Expressive Design, Sanzo Wada Japanese Color Harmonies, and astronomical precision (Surya Siddhanta & Lahiri Ephemeris).

## Core Capabilities
1. **5 Limbs of Panchanga**: Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Vaara with sub-minute transitions.
2. **Topocentric Ephemeris**: Latitude, longitude, and elevation-aware Sunrise, Sunset, Moonrise, Moonset, and Auspicious Muhurtas (Abhijit, Brahma, Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda).
3. **Choghadiya & Hora**: 8-fold Day and Night divisions with ticking countdown timers and planetary Hora cycles.
4. **Triple Kundali Charts**: North Indian Diamond, South Indian Square, and East Indian Bangla formats with 6-fold Shadbala and Sarvashtakavarga (SAV).
5. **16-Petal Vedic Vastu Compass**: Hardware magnetometer & gyroscope integration with 8 Ashtadikpalas and architectural guidance.
6. **60-Ghati Hindu Clock**: Divides solar day from Sunrise into Ghatis, Palas, Vipalas, and exact Ishta Kaal.
7. **Sanzo Wada Harmonies**: 8 palettes inspired by Sanzo Wada's 1930s dictionary (*A Dictionary of Color Combinations*).
8. **Desktop Home Widgets**: Transparent 4x4 Month Grid & 4x2 Daily Panchang widgets.
9. **Offline Global Cities**: 4,500+ compressed cities with coordinates and timezones (`assets/cities.json.gz`).

## Target Audience
- Astrologers, Vedic scholars, and temple pujaris seeking authentic topocentric timekeeping.
- Seekers observing Ekadashi, Pradosh, and Sanatana Dharma vrats.
- Android users desiring privacy-first, zero-ad, battery-efficient spiritual utilities.

## Technology Stack
- **Native Android**: Java (JDK 11+), Android API 26–35.
- **Standalone Build System**: AAPT2, D8, Zipalign, Apksigner via PowerShell (`CustomHinduWidget/build.ps1`).
- **Web Showcase**: Three.js (r128), Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Google Fonts (`Outfit`, `Cinzel`, `Tiro Devanagari Hindi`).
