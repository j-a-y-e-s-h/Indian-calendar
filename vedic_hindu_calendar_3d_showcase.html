पंचांग गणना, अमांत बनाम पूर्णिमांत परंपरा एवं ऑफ़लाइन सटीकता से जुड़े प्रश्न

In the Amanta (अमांत) system followed across Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Tamil Nadu, each lunar month ends on No Moon (Amavasya). In the Purnimanta (पूर्णिमांत) system followed across North, Central, and Eastern India, the lunar month ends on Full Moon (Purnima). Both traditions share identical Shukla Paksha dates, while Krishna Paksha falls under different month names. You can toggle effortlessly between Amanta and Purnimanta inside the Settings Modal.
Unlike generic apps that use fixed 6:00 AM–6:00 PM approximations, our engine computes exact Drik Ganita topocentric Sunrise (Udaya) and Sunset (Asta) using your city's latitude, longitude, elevation, and atmospheric refraction correction. Daytime length (Dinamana = Sunset − Sunrise) is divided into 8 equal daytime Choghadiya Muhurtas, while nighttime length (Ratrimana = Next Sunrise − Sunset) is divided into 8 equal nocturnal Choghadiyas.
The app embeds high-precision analytical planetary perturbation series (VSOP87 & ELP2000-82 lunar theory) paired with true Lahiri (Chitrapaksha), Raman, and KP Ayanamsa models directly in native code. Alongside a compressed spatial database of 4,500+ global cities and IANA timezones, the entire APK stays just ~1 MB and runs 100% offline forever—even in remote Himalayan temples or during flights.
